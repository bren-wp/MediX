## 💊 Everything important, beautifully connected

Medicines carry more than a name. MediX helps you explore the details that matter — active ingredients, strength, pharmaceutical forms, packaging, dispensing status, reimbursement information and available prices — without switching between multiple disconnected resources.

**Built for clinicians, understandable to everyone.** Each medicine profile now opens with a concise prescription and HZZO coverage summary. Where a verified HZZO copayment exists for the matched original pack, its amount and source date appear clearly. Mandatory insurance, supplementary health insurance, the HZZO Supplementary List, wholesale prices and pharmacy retail prices are never conflated. Unknown coverage or retail prices stay explicitly unknown instead of becoming guessed amounts.


The medicine identity comes from the **official HALMED medicine catalog**. HZZO reimbursement information and separate pricing sources complement those records without replacing or inventing medicine names.

## ⚡ One-tap professional medicine lookup

Combine quick filters for prescription-only, OTC, HZZO list status and verified copayments without losing previous choices or deeper clinical filters. Tap an active filter to remove only that condition, or use **All** to clear all filter restrictions while preserving the search text and selected sort. This is useful when checking, for example, prescription medicines on the HZZO Supplementary List with a confirmed copayment.

The home screen has adaptive tile heights for 320px Android layouts and users who enable larger system text. The same actions remain available without horizontal navigation.

## 🩺 Prescription status, with evidence

MediX recognizes documented Croatian dispensing codes **BR / BRX** (without prescription) and **R / RS** (prescription), as well as unambiguous status text in official records. Contradictory, negated or unrecognized labels are **not guessed**: they remain unverified in the search filters, medicine profiles and comparisons. The exact incoming source text remains available in the professional metadata for verification.

The medicine's dispensing status is distinct from reimbursement and individual insurance coverage. Always check the latest official documentation for the exact medicine and package.

## ↔️ Safer comparisons and transparent wholesale price lists

Compare up to four medicines, including prescription, package, HZZO list, effective-date and price-source differences. Only verified HZZO copayments and finite nonnegative EUR wholesale amounts are displayed. A medicine without a confirmed HZZO record is **not automatically outside coverage**.

The separate published-price catalog explicitly identifies **maximum wholesale prices**, not personal pharmacy checkout prices. Invalid amounts and missing source dates are treated as unknown, never as zero, a fake date or a retail quote. Search entries are prepared when the catalog loads for smoother repeated lookup.

## 🛡️ Source-aware clinical data

MediX keeps the effective date and source of a **confirmed HZZO list match** with the specific medicine and package. When an official effective date is missing, a dated HZZO copayment is not invented. The medicine screen explains the missing date rather than passing it off as current. Official prices are historical/offline snapshots, not live pharmacy quotes.

Medicine search also prepares its normalized catalog text once per search screen, making repeated queries more efficient without changing the meaning of medicines, strengths, ATC terms or filters.

## 🔎 Fast, clinically useful medicine search

Search by medicine name, active ingredient, ATC code or package and use one-tap filters for prescription status, non-prescription medicines, HZZO Basic List, HZZO Supplementary List and **confirmed** HZZO copayments. Further professional filters remain available when needed.

Price sorting never ranks HZZO copayments against wholesale prices. Each is a different measure with its own ascending/descending sort. Missing or unverified HZZO list matches are labeled *not confirmed*, not *not listed*. No retail pharmacy price or individual supplementary-insurance coverage is inferred.

## 📑 Official medicine information, without guesswork

Open the current medicine record and find prescription status, HZZO coverage, packaging and available prices together. Where official links are supplied, MediX can open the patient leaflet or professional Summary of Product Characteristics. If a specific document is not available, an explicit HALMED registry link lets you verify the exact medicine and strength yourself.

MediX takes a conservative approach to reimbursement data: conflicting strengths or known package counts do **not** receive another product's copayment or wholesale price. Missing prices remain marked as unavailable rather than invented. The HZZO Supplementary List and a person's supplementary health-insurance policy remain separate concepts.

## 📅 A calmer way to organize therapy

**Make your schedule work for you.** Add medicines, record your instructions, select the right days of the week and set reminder times. Review upcoming doses in the therapy calendar, pause reminders when needed, and update your plan without starting again. MediX validates reminder times before saving and keeps rapid schedule changes in order.

Your personal therapy information remains on your device. You do not need an account to use the core MediX experience.

## 🩺 Health information, handled responsibly

MediX is an informational medicine and clinical-calculation companion. It is **not a medical device**, does not diagnose or prescribe treatment, and is not a replacement for a qualified healthcare professional. A missing interaction record must never be interpreted as confirmation that a medicine combination is safe. Always refer to the official product information and clinical advice before making treatment decisions.
