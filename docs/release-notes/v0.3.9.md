# MediX v0.3.9

This release focuses on accuracy and trust when interpreting medicine records, reimbursements, and professional documents.

## Safer pack-level reimbursement and pricing

- HZZO copayment and wholesale pricing enrichment now rejects conflicting known medicine strengths and package unit counts.
- If a source supplies a strength or recognizable pack quantity but the corresponding HALMED record does not establish the same value, MediX avoids an uncertain match.
- Matching checks the product name before performing the more expensive strength and package parsing.
- Negative or non-finite official prices are rejected at import.
- Both the expandable price records and the copyable medicine summary use the same validated price visibility rules as the insurance overview.
- No pharmacy retail price or insurance co-payment entitlement is guessed.

## Official medicine documents

- Medicine details provide access to official medicine information from the HALMED register.
- When supplied in the catalog, medicine-specific patient leaflet and Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) links can be opened directly.
- The app accepts only HTTPS links on trusted HALMED, EMA and HZZO domains for these actions.
- When product-specific document links are absent, the HALMED register is clearly identified as a search destination rather than a fabricated medicine PDF.
- Source-supplied `smpc_url` and `patient_leaflet_url` fields are preserved by the HALMED model importer when present.
- New documents UI translations are available in Croatian and English with the existing English fallback.

## Verification

Regressions cover mismatched strengths, different pack sizes, insufficient source packaging, malicious links, document buttons, and the integrity of HZZO copayment representations. Analyze, unit/widget tests, production Dart reachability audit, debug APK and release APK/AAB builds are required before merging and publishing.
