# MediX v0.3.13

MediX v0.3.13 improves the accuracy of prescription and non-prescription labels across the Croatian medicine catalog.

## Accurate dispensing status interpretation

- Interpret documented **BR** and **BRX** dispensing codes as non-prescription, and **R** and **RS** dispensing codes as prescription-only.
- Recognize explicit source phrases such as `bez recepta`, `na recept` and `uz recept`.
- Keep contradictory status metadata, negated instructions and unrecognized abbreviations **unverified** rather than deciding a medicine can be bought without a prescription.
- Apply the same logic to both HALMED and HZZO catalog imports.
- Preserve the original incoming text in the professional record for checking against official medicine documents.
- Propagate conservative status handling to the medicine profile, search filters, comparison screen and copyable medical summary.

## Regression testing

- BR / BRX / R / RS status code variants, including accompanying descriptive text.
- Explicit Croatian dispensing phrases, contradictory codes, mixed prescription/OTC claims and negated phrases.
- Unknown external abbreviations do not become non-prescription without positive evidence.
- Source import regression verifying that search filters and labels agree for four different official medicine records.
- All existing HZZO price matching, copayment, documented source, dispensing, and clinical regression tests must continue to pass.

## Medical safety

A product's marketing-authorization dispensing rules are distinct from its HZZO reimbursement terms and the insured patient's individual coverage. Dispensing may vary with product strength, route, or packaging; MediX does not infer permission to buy a product without a prescription. Clinicians and patients should verify official product documents and pharmacy dispensing rules before acting on these data.

Sources: HALMED medicine registration database (https://www.halmed.hr/Lijekovi/Baza-lijekova/) and official Croatian prescription regulations (https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/full/2024_03_37_597.html).
