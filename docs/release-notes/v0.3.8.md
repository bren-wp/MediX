# MediX v0.3.8

This release makes official Croatian medicine information **faster to interpret for clinicians** and easier to understand for everyone else.

## Prescription & insurance at a glance

- A medicine's detail page begins with clear prescription or non-prescription information, based on the source medicine record.
- HZZO Basic List and Supplementary List status is described separately from the patient's **supplementary health-insurance policy**.
- When an explicitly matched HZZO Supplementary List record provides a copayment for an original pack, MediX displays the amount, source and effective date.
- Where HZZO list coverage, copayment or pharmacy retail pricing cannot be confirmed, MediX says so clearly.
- A published maximum wholesale price is **never** presented as the amount payable in a pharmacy.
- Users can open the official HZZO published medicine lists directly from the medicine view.

## Better daily workflow

- A cleaner, more compact medicine header leaves more room for clinical facts.
- Professional/regulatory details and additional price records are available in expandable sections.
- Official HZZO copayment information, when verified, appears directly in the medicine search card.
- Croatian and English explanations with an English fallback for other locales.

## Medical and financial accuracy

- No medicine data, retail prices, or insurance benefits have been invented.
- A missing HZZO match does not establish that a medicine is excluded from reimbursement.
- No assumption is made that supplementary health insurance results in a zero-euro payment.
- HZZO reimbursement is subject to the relevant prescribing conditions, coverage rules and specific pack.

## Regression and release gates

Regression tests cover prescription displays, HZZO list distinctions, price-type separation, invalid prices, unknown prices and localization. The release is published only after Flutter analyze, tests, Dart reachability checks, Android CI and APK/AAB release builds complete successfully.
